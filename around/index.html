Weather to expect

Late Danish summer — mild, breezy and changeable.

21° by day, 12° at night

Light from about 06:00 to 20:45

Rain on roughly one day in three, usually showers rather than all day

Pack: Layers, a proper rain jacket each, and one warm top for the evenings. Shorts and a fleece will both get used.
