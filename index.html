Worth knowing

  • Netto is 4 minutes away and open 07:00–22:00 every day, so the Saturday evening shop is not a problem.
  • 500 EUR does not stretch to BOTH WOW PARK and the Lalandia Aquadome. Adding the Aquadome (~800 DKK) puts you roughly 45 EUR over before anything else.
  • The LEGOLAND Resort Pass is only sold with a stay at LEGOLAND Hotel or Lalandia, so it is not available on a private booking. Buy dated individual tickets.
  • Lalandia lockers work off your wristband — no 20 DKK coin needed.
  • Book WOW PARK on a weekday tier (199 DKK rather than 299) — about 40 EUR saved for three.
  • Worth confirming: whether LEGOLAND lets you bring your own food. Four days of in-park meals is the biggest unknown in the budget.
  • Arriving 15:45 on the 15th — the first evening is transfer, check-in and the shop, not a park.
  • Return flight is 18:45 on the 22nd, so the last day is free until late afternoon.
  • DKK is pegged to the euro, so 7.46 DKK to 1 EUR holds all week.
